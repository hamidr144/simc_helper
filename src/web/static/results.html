Honest absences

No probability badges: this engine simulates each candidate as its own actor and reports DPS, ±error and statistical ties — it emits no per-item probability badges or talent recommendations, so this page shows none.
Combinations: the engine simulates each bundle as one actor — the delta belongs to the whole combination and no per-item value exists inside it, so combination details and the full-set view list items, ilvl and what they replace only.
Gems & enchants: dashed rec chips and the full-set recommended-gems line come from enhancement recommendations (app config), not per-combo output — slots whose config entry is empty show nothing.

How to read this

DPS error bars come from the simulator; rows within 2× of the best row's error are statistical ties ("≈ best") even when their DPS differs. Simulated runs that failed or changed no gear are never ranked.
